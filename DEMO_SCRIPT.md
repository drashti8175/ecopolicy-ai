# EcoPolicy AI - Demo Video Script (2-5 Minutes)

## Title: Modeling Urban Sustainability in Ahmedabad with EcoPolicy AI

---

### [0:00 - 0:45] 1. Introduction & Problem Statement
- **Visual**: Landing page of the EcoPolicy AI Dashboard.
- **Voiceover**: "Welcome to EcoPolicy AI, an advanced urban sustainability simulation platform designed for city planners, policymakers, and environmental analysts. Rapid urbanization across Indian cities like Delhi, Ahmedabad, and Surat has put immense pressure on air quality, energy grids, and carbon footprints. Traditional planning tools are static and disconnected. EcoPolicy AI provides an instant, interactive simulation environment to model green policy decisions before they are enacted."

### [0:45 - 2:00] 2. Live Simulation Demo (Ahmedabad EV & Solar Scenario)
- **Visual**: Selecting "Ahmedabad" from the city selector dropdown. Adjusting EV adoption slider to 45% and Solar adoption slider to 50%. Clicking "Run Simulation".
- **Voiceover**: "Let's take Ahmedabad as our testbed. Suppose the municipal corporation wants to evaluate the impact of aggressive green policies by 2030—specifically scaling EV adoption to 45% and Rooftop Solar to 50%, alongside moderate increases in tree cover and public transport. When we hit 'Run Simulation', our backend engine instantly runs regression models trained on historical CPCB and energy data."
- **Visual**: Highlighting the 4 Result Metric Cards (CO₂ emissions down by 28.5%, AQI improved by 35 points, Green Score rising from 52 to 78/100).
- **Voiceover**: "Notice the real-time delta indicators. CO₂ emissions drop significantly, air quality index improves, and while electricity demand rises due to EV charging, the massive solar adoption keeps the grid balanced, culminating in a Green Score jump of 26 points."

### [2:00 - 3:00] 3. AI Insights & Scenario Comparison
- **Visual**: Scrolling down to the AI Insights Panel showing the gpt-5-mini generated explanation, then saving the scenario and opening the Scenario Manager to compare with baseline.
- **Voiceover**: "Below the metrics, our integrated AI Insights panel—powered by advanced LLMs—delivers a concise, professional policy summary tailored to Ahmedabad's specific context. Furthermore, planners can save multiple scenarios and use the Scenario Comparison tool to evaluate trade-offs side-by-side with Recharts visualizations."

### [3:00 - 4:00] 4. Report Generation & Conclusion
- **Visual**: Clicking "Download Report" to generate and download the professional PDF summary report, then showcasing the architecture.
- **Voiceover**: "Finally, analysts can instantly export a comprehensive PDF report containing all simulation parameters, metric changes, and AI recommendations for stakeholder meetings. EcoPolicy AI bridges the gap between raw data and actionable climate policy, supporting UN Sustainable Development Goals 7, 11, and 13. Thank you for watching."
